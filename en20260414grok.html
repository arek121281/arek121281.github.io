Scientists discover breakthrough in heart tissue regeneration

Test dla klasy 2 liceum • Multiple Choice + Polish translations

By Al Jazeera Staff • Published: March 2025

Researchers at the University of Cambridge have announced a significant medical breakthrough that could transform treatment for heart disease. Using a combination of stem cells and artificial intelligence, scientists have successfully regenerated damaged heart tissue in laboratory conditions.

Heart disease remains the leading cause of death worldwide. Unlike other organs, the human heart has very limited natural ability to repair itself. When heart muscle is damaged after a heart attack, it is usually replaced by scar tissue, which increases the risk of future heart failure.

The new technique involves reprogramming adult stem cells into heart muscle cells with the help of AI algorithms that can predict the most effective molecular combinations. In laboratory tests, the regenerated tissue showed electrical and mechanical properties very similar to natural heart muscle.

“This is not just about growing cells. It’s about creating functional heart tissue that can integrate with the patient’s own heart,” said Dr. Elena Petrova, lead researcher. The team hopes that clinical trials on humans could begin within the next five years.

The discovery has been welcomed by cardiologists around the world, though some experts warn that turning laboratory success into real-world treatment is often more difficult than expected.
